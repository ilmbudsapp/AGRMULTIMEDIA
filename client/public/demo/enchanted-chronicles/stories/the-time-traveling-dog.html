Story #11

The Time-Traveling Dog

In the medieval village of Eldoria, on a cold winter morning, a dog named Cubi awoke with an unusual feeling. Though he seemed like an ordinary dog, his life was anything but ordinary. His owner, Bertrand—a renowned alchemist—had spent years working on a mysterious time‑traveling device.

Eldoria was a quaint village of cobblestone streets and thatched‑roof cottages. Smoke curled from chimneys, the smell of fresh bread filled the air, horses trotted down narrow lanes, and children played in the snow, their laughter echoing through the crisp morning.

Bertrand’s workshop, tucked away at the village’s edge, was a place of wonder. Shelves overflowed with ancient tomes and peculiar artifacts. Bottles of strange potions and odd mechanical devices cluttered every surface. Bertrand, with his long white beard and spectacles perched on his nose, muttered to himself as he adjusted gears and levers on his latest invention.

Cubi loved being in the workshop. He often curled up in a corner, watching Bertrand’s experiments with curious eyes. Bertrand would pause occasionally to scratch Cubi behind the ears. “One day, Cubi,” he’d say with a twinkle, “this device will change the world.”

Despite his fascination, Cubi was playful. He darted around the workshop, chasing shadows and scraps of parchment. His energy brought warmth to Bertrand’s solitary work.

One evening, as twilight bathed the village in soft gold, Bertrand made a breakthrough. “I’ve done it, Cubi!” he exclaimed. “The time‑travel device is complete!”

He placed the final piece—a shimmering crystal—into the machine. It hummed to life, sparks dancing around it. Cubi watched in awe.

“Let’s test it,” Bertrand said, adjusting the dials.

But before he could activate it, a shelf overloaded with books collapsed. Startled, Cubi leapt forward, knocking over a vial of potion. The liquid spilled onto the device, causing it to spark violently.

A blinding flash filled the room. The workshop shook. Cubi felt himself pulled into a vortex of swirling colors and deafening noise.

Then—silence.

Cubi opened his eyes and found himself lying in a forest clearing. The trees were tall and unfamiliar. Birds chirped overhead. He was no longer in Eldoria.

He wandered through the forest until he reached the outskirts of a modern city. Towering buildings, neon lights, roaring engines—everything was strange. But Cubi was determined to find his way home.

Amid the chaos, he approached a young woman at a coffee cart. Her name was Sarah, a physics student obsessed with time‑travel theories. She immediately noticed Cubi’s odd appearance—medieval armor mixed with modern elements.

“Where did you come from?” she whispered, kneeling.

Cubi sensed her kindness and leaned into her touch. Sarah took him to her apartment—a cozy space filled with books, gadgets, and tools. She worked tirelessly to understand the device that had brought Cubi through time.

Days turned into weeks. Sarah and Cubi became inseparable. She took him on walks through the city, explaining everything she could. Cubi marveled at skyscrapers, markets, and glowing screens.

Together, they reconstructed Bertrand’s device. When it was finally ready, Sarah activated it. A brilliant light filled the room, and Cubi felt himself pulled through time once more.

He landed in a dense forest—different from Eldoria, different from the modern world. After wandering, he reached a strange hybrid village where medieval cottages stood beside sleek modern buildings. People wore a mix of old and new clothing.

An elderly man named Alaric—a historian fascinated by time travel—recognized Cubi immediately.

“You must be the time‑traveling dog I’ve heard whispers about.”

Alaric explained that time fractures—disturbances caused by misused time‑travel devices—had merged eras together. Cubi, carrying the energy of Bertrand’s invention, was the key to fixing them.

Guided by Alaric, Cubi explored the village. In the central square stood a grand clock tower. Inside, he discovered hidden chambers filled with ancient manuscripts and advanced technology.

At the tower’s heart, Cubi found the Chrono Crystal—a glowing artifact capable of mending time fractures. When he touched it, the tower’s mechanisms sprang to life. Time began to realign.

But the fractures were widespread. Cubi traveled across the hybrid village, repairing rifts with the crystal’s power. Along the way, he met Elara—a young inventor whose knowledge of ancient and modern science proved invaluable.

Together, they journeyed to an abandoned castle where a massive fracture warped reality. Using Elara’s gadgets and the Chrono Crystal, they sealed the rift. Slowly, the world returned to normal.

With the timeline stabilized, Cubi focused on returning home. Elara built a portal using the crystal’s energy. Cubi stepped through—and found himself back in Bertrand’s workshop.

Bertrand, older now, stared in disbelief. “Cubi… you’re back.”

He knelt and embraced his loyal companion.

The villagers soon learned of Cubi’s adventures. Bertrand’s workshop became a hub for scholars studying time. Eldoria held an annual Chrono Festival in Cubi’s honor.

As years passed, Bertrand’s health declined. He entrusted Cubi to Lydia, a brilliant young apprentice. Together, Lydia and Cubi continued Bertrand’s work.

One day, Lydia discovered a manuscript describing the Eternal Hourglass—a powerful artifact capable of safeguarding time itself. She and Cubi embarked on a new quest.

Their journey led them through ancient ruins and treacherous landscapes. Finally, they reached a hidden temple deep in the mountains. After solving intricate puzzles, they found the Eternal Hourglass glowing in golden light.

Lydia touched it, feeling its immense power. “With this,” she whispered, “we can protect the timeline forever.”

Cubi barked softly, ready for whatever came next.

Their adventure was far from over. But together, they would continue exploring the mysteries of time—ensuring that Bertrand’s legacy lived on, and that the world remained safe from temporal chaos.
