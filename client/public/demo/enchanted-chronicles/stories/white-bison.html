Story #6

White Bison

In a small, impoverished village at the edge of an ancient forest, the people lived in constant struggle. Their crops had failed for three consecutive seasons, the once fertile soil had turned barren, and the river that sustained them had dried up. Day by day, the villagers grew weaker, and hope began to fade.

Amid this despair, a young man named Lian emerged as a beacon of courage. Known for his kindness and determination, he always helped others despite the hardships he faced. He lived with his elderly parents, who had taught him perseverance and compassion.

One evening, as the village elders gathered to discuss their dire situation, Lian stood up. “We cannot continue like this,” he said firmly. “We must find a way to save our village.”

The elders nodded, but their faces were heavy with worry. “What can we do, Lian? We have tried everything we know,” one elder replied.

Lian recalled an ancient legend his grandmother once told him. “There is a legend of a White Bison deep within the forest,” he said. “It is said to possess great wisdom and grant guidance to those in need. If we can find it, perhaps it can help us.”

The villagers whispered among themselves—fearful, yet intrigued. The forest was dangerous, filled with unknown creatures and treacherous paths. Many who ventured into its depths never returned.

Despite the risks, Lian was resolute. “I will go into the forest and seek the White Bison. It is our only hope.”

His parents, though worried, supported him. “Be careful, my son,” his mother said, embracing him. “The future of our village rests on your shoulders.”

At dawn, armed with only a small satchel and unwavering determination, Lian set off. The villagers watched him disappear into the forest, their eyes filled with fear and hope.

The forest was unlike anything Lian had ever seen—towering trees with twisted branches, strange glowing plants, and distant calls of unseen creatures. Every step was cautious, but his resolve never faltered.

Days turned into weeks. Lian crossed swift rivers, climbed steep cliffs, and avoided wild animals. Each challenge strengthened his determination.

One evening, he stumbled upon a secluded hut where an old hermit lived. The hermit welcomed him kindly.

“You seek the White Bison,” the hermit said. “Many have tried, few have succeeded. The path is perilous.”

“I must find it,” Lian replied. “My village depends on it.”

The hermit nodded. “I will guide you as far as I can. But the final steps must be taken alone.”

Together, they traveled through enchanted groves, shimmering streams, and rugged hills. The hermit shared wisdom about patience, courage, and the secrets of the forest.

Finally, they reached a sacred clearing. “This is where I leave you,” the hermit said. “The White Bison comes only to those who are worthy.”

Lian stepped into the clearing, knelt, and prayed for guidance.

At dawn, he heard a gentle rustling. When he opened his eyes, a magnificent White Bison stood before him—gigantic, radiant, and awe‑inspiring. Its fur glowed with pure white light, its eyes deep with ancient wisdom, and its horns curved with majestic strength.

“Greetings, young traveler,” the bison said in a deep, resonant voice. “I am the guardian of this forest. Why have you come?”

Lian gathered his courage. “My village is dying. Our crops have failed, our river is gone, and my people suffer. I seek your wisdom to save them.”

The bison studied him carefully. “Your quest is noble. Many have sought my counsel, but few are worthy. You must prove your courage, wisdom, and compassion.”

“I am ready,” Lian said.

“Then you must complete three trials,” the bison said. “Only then will you earn the knowledge to save your people.”

The First Trial — The Sacred Water

“To restore life, you must retrieve the Sacred Water from the Enchanted Spring,” the bison said. “It is guarded by the Spirits of the Forest.”

Lian followed the path of the setting sun until he reached the glowing spring. As he approached, dark spirits emerged.

“You shall not take the water,” one hissed.

“I must save my village,” Lian replied.

The spirits attacked. Lian fought bravely, using agility and calmness taught by the hermit. After a fierce battle, he reached the spring and filled his flask. The spirits retreated into the shadows.

He returned to the clearing. “You have shown courage,” the bison said. “You have passed the first trial.”

The Second Trial — The Heartstone

“Your next trial is to retrieve the Heartstone from the Cave of Echoes,” the bison said. “It is guarded by the Echo Serpent, a creature that feeds on fear.”

Lian traveled north through rugged mountains until he reached the cave. Inside, glowing fungi lit the darkness. The Echo Serpent appeared—massive, stone‑scaled, and menacing.

“You dare enter my domain?” it hissed.

“I seek the Heartstone to save my village,” Lian replied.

“Only those who conquer their deepest fears may claim it.”

Lian confronted the serpent with unwavering resolve. Each moment of bravery weakened the creature. In a final struggle, he seized the Heartstone. The serpent dissolved into darkness.

He returned to the clearing. “You have shown strength of spirit,” the bison said. “You have passed the second trial.”

The Third Trial — The Flame of Hope

“Your final trial,” the bison said, “is to retrieve the Flame of Hope from the Peak of Peril. It is guarded by the Fire Guardian.”

Lian climbed the treacherous mountain—icy slopes, fierce winds, thin air. At the summit, he entered a cavern glowing with fire.

The Fire Guardian appeared—lava‑scaled, blazing‑eyed, fierce.

“Only those with unwavering resolve may claim the Flame of Hope.”

“I will save my village,” Lian said.

The guardian unleashed torrents of fire. Lian dodged, stayed calm, and pressed forward. In a final burst of courage, he reached the Flame of Hope and grasped it. The guardian bowed.

“You are worthy.”

The Return

Lian returned to the clearing. “You have completed all three trials,” the bison said. “Use these gifts wisely.”

With the Sacred Water, Heartstone, and Flame of Hope, Lian returned to his village. He restored the river, rejuvenated the soil, and brought warmth and light back to his people.

The village flourished once more. The villagers celebrated Lian’s bravery and honored the White Bison.

Lian had saved his people—and discovered strength and wisdom within himself.

Epilogue — The Wisdom of the White Bison

Deep in the ancient forest, the White Bison continued to guard the land’s secrets. Lian’s story became a legend—a testament to courage, determination, and the spirit of a true hero.
