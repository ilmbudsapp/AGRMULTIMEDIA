Canon

Micro-Canon Blocks

Guardian Emergence

Guardians first appear at sacred waters when purity of heart meets perseverance. Lyria at the Misty Lake is the foundational guardian event of the published canon.

Redemption Law

Sir Agron's compassion toward the giant of Aranthia establishes that understanding can succeed where battle fails. This becomes moral canon across the universe.

Hope Magic

Billy's awakening in Geislingen proves that community belief is a form of magic. Village hope is recognised as a canonical force.

Standalone Principle

Every chronicle is complete in itself. No reading order is canonically required — accessibility is a universe law.

Magic Reward System

Magic responds to courage, kindness, and perseverance — never to greed, cruelty, or impatience. This rule applies universe-wide.

Spirit-Animal Canon

Sacred animals appear at moments of crisis or renewal. The white bison and white wolf are the two primary spirit-animal entities.
